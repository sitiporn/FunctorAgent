International Travel Policy

Employees traveling internationally for business purposes must obtain
manager approval before booking flights or accommodation. International
business trips should be submitted at least 14 days before the planned
departure date. Economy-class airfare is reimbursable for trips shorter
than six hours, while business class may be approved for flights longer
than six hours.

Travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days after returning from
the trip. Employees should keep receipts for accommodation, transportation,
and other reimbursable expenses. Personal expenses are not eligible for
reimbursement.

Annual Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 15 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Leave requests should normally be submitted to the manager at least
three working days in advance. Employees should coordinate with their
team before taking extended leave to ensure adequate work coverage.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
Employees working remotely are expected to remain available during normal
working hours and attend required meetings. Certain customer-facing
positions may have additional on-site requirements.

Laptop Replacement Policy

Company laptops are normally replaced every four years. Employees whose
devices experience hardware problems should contact the IT support team.
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT and the employee's manager
as soon as possible.
